CAEG Syllabus

This subject develops Engineering Graphics skills through drawing, projections, curves, scales, solids, and surface development.

Unit-Wise Syllabus

  1. Unit I — Curves and Scales
    Conic sections: parabola, hyperbola, and ellipse. Cycloidal curves: cycloid, hypocycloid, and epicycloid. Plane scales and diagonal scales.
  2. Unit II — Projections of Points and Lines
    Principles of projection, projections of points in different quadrants, and projections of straight lines inclined to reference planes.
  3. Unit III — Projections of Planes
    Projections of plane figures and their positions with respect to horizontal plane and vertical plane.
  4. Unit IV — Projections of Solids and Development of Surfaces
    Projections of solids such as prisms, pyramids, cylinders, cones, and development of surfaces.
  5. Unit V — Conventions and Compound Solids
    Engineering drawing conventions and projections of compound solids.

Quick CAEG Notes

Conic Section

A conic section is a curve obtained when a plane cuts a cone. Examples are ellipse, parabola, and hyperbola.

Scale

A scale is used to draw an object larger or smaller than its actual size.

Projection

Projection is the representation of a three-dimensional object on a two-dimensional drawing plane.

Development of Surface

Development of surfaces means unfolding the surface of a solid onto a plane.

After Completing CAEG, You Will Be Able To

  • Draw conic sections and cycloidal curves.
  • Use plane and diagonal scales in engineering drawings.
  • Draw projections of points, lines, and planes.
  • Draw projections of solids and develop their surfaces.
  • Apply standard engineering drawing conventions.

Sample Drawing Activity

Steps to Draw an Ellipse

  1. Draw the major axis and minor axis.
  2. Mark the centre point where both axes meet.
  3. Mark equal divisions on the axes.
  4. Plot points using the chosen construction method.
  5. Join the points smoothly to form an ellipse.
