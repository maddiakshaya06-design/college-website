BEEE Syllabus

This course introduces the basics of electrical and electronics engineering for first-year students.

Unit-Wise Syllabus

  1. Unit I — Semiconductor Devices
    Introduction to semiconductors, intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors, p-n junction diode, rectifiers, transistors, LEDs, and basic electronic devices.
  2. Unit II — Number System and Boolean Algebra
    Decimal, binary, octal, and hexadecimal number systems, number conversions, binary arithmetic, Boolean laws, Boolean expressions, and simplification.
  3. Unit III — Logic Gates
    AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, XOR, and XNOR gates, truth tables, universal gates, and basic logic-circuit design.
  4. Unit IV — DC and AC Circuits
    Ohm’s law, Kirchhoff’s laws, series and parallel circuits, DC circuits, AC waveform, RMS value, power factor, and basic AC circuit analysis.
  5. Unit V — Electrical Machines
    Transformer, DC generator, DC motor, induction motor, synchronous machines, and applications of electrical machines.

Quick BEEE Notes

Semiconductor

A semiconductor has conductivity between a conductor and an insulator. Silicon is a commonly used semiconductor material.

Boolean Algebra

Boolean algebra uses only two values: 0 and 1. It is used to design digital logic circuits.

Logic Gate

A logic gate takes one or more binary inputs and produces one binary output.

Ohm's Law

Ohm’s law states that V = I × R, where V is voltage, I is current, and R is resistance.

After Completing BEEE, You Will Be Able To

  • Understand the basic working of semiconductor devices.
  • Perform number-system conversions and Boolean simplification.
  • Use logic gates and truth tables.
  • Apply basic laws to DC and AC circuits.
  • Identify common electrical machines and their uses.

Unit 4 — DC and AC Circuits
