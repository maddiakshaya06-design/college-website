Matrices and Calculus Syllabus

This subject covers matrices, eigenvalues, single-variable functions, multiple differentiation, and multiple integration.

Unit-Wise Syllabus

  1. Unit I — Real and Complex Matrices
    Rank of a matrix, echelon form, normal or canonical form, inverse matrix, system of homogeneous equations (AX = 0), system of non-homogeneous equations (AX = B), and Gauss-Seidel method.
  2. Unit II — Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors
    Eigenvalues and eigenvectors, diagonal matrix, orthogonal matrix, Cayley-Hamilton theorem, quadratic form by canonical form, and quadratic form by orthogonal form.
  3. Unit III — Single-Variable Functions
    Rolle’s theorem, Lagrange’s Mean Value Theorem, Cauchy’s Mean Value Theorem, Taylor series, and Maclaurin series.
  4. Unit IV — Multiple Calculus: Differentiation
    Basic partial differential equation problems, chain rule, Euler’s theorem, Jacobian, and maxima and minima of two variables.
  5. Unit V — Multiple Calculus: Integration
    Basic double and triple integration problems, changing the order of integration in Cartesian and polar coordinates, and finding area and volume using double integrals.

Quick Mathematics Notes

Matrix

A matrix is a rectangular arrangement of numbers in rows and columns.

Eigenvalue

If AX = λX, then λ is called an eigenvalue and X is called its corresponding eigenvector.

Rolle's Theorem

If a function is continuous and differentiable in a given interval, then there is at least one point where its derivative is zero.

Partial Differentiation

Partial differentiation means differentiating a function with respect to one variable while keeping the other variables constant.

Double Integration

Double integration is used to find the area of a region or volume under a surface.

After Completing Mathematics, You Will Be Able To

  • Solve matrix equations and find the rank and inverse of matrices.
  • Find eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a matrix.
  • Apply mean value theorems and series expansions.
  • Solve partial differentiation and maxima-minima problems.
  • Use double and triple integration to find area and volume.

Important Formulas — All Units

Unit I — Matrices

For a 2 × 2 matrix A = [a b; c d]:
det(A) = ad − bc

A⁻¹ = 1/(ad − bc) [d −b; −c a]

Unit II — Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors

Characteristic equation:
det(A − λI) = 0

Eigenvector equation:
AX = λX

Unit III — Single-Variable Functions

Taylor series:
f(x) = f(a) + (x − a)f′(a) + (x − a)²/2! f″(a) + ...

Maclaurin series is Taylor series at a = 0.

Unit IV — Multiple Calculus: Differentiation

Total derivative:
dz/dx = ∂z/∂x + ∂z/∂y × dy/dx

Euler’s theorem:
x(∂z/∂x) + y(∂z/∂y) = nz

Unit V — Multiple Calculus: Integration

Double integration:
∬ f(x, y) dx dy

Area of a region R:
A = ∬R 1 dA

Volume under a surface:
V = ∬R z dA
